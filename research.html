From-Scratch 740M Foundation Model: Convergence Stability, Custom Tokenizer Design, and GQA Edge Scaling on A100 Silicon

Abstract — Most sub-billion parameter models on the market are superficial API wrappers or LoRA fine-tunes layered over third-party open checkpoints. In this report, we present the complete design and pre-training verification of a sovereign 740-million-parameter decoder-only transformer trained from scratch on A100 infrastructure across 4 billion curated tokens.

By combining Grouped-Query Attention (GQA), FlashAttention-2, Rotary Position Embeddings (RoPE), and SwiGLU activations with a syntax-aware custom tokenizer, the model achieves stable bfloat16 convergence. When quantized to 4-bit Q4_K_M (GGUF), the runtime occupies only ~1 GB of local RAM while sustaining 40+ tokens/sec completely offline—powering deterministic function calling, repository diff analysis, and step-by-step Chain-of-Thought (CoT) agent workflows with zero external data leakage.
