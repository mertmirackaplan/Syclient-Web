01 / Company definition

Runtime first. Model work in support of the agent.

Our current focus is Mosaic Agent: a local Close Beta runtime for planning, tool use, execution, verification, and self-repair. Our model work exists to improve the quality, efficiency, and reliability of those agent workflows.

We do not describe Syclient as a processor company today. “Software before silicon” remains a sequencing principle; any future hardware work would follow proven software requirements and be presented as a separate research stage.

02 / Team

Small enough to own the whole system.

Syclient is a founder-led, independent technical team. We work end to end across agent orchestration, local inference, memory, verification, product design, and small-scale model research.

That structure keeps the feedback loop short: a failure found in a Close Beta workflow can become a runtime test, a verification rule, a memory constraint, or a model experiment without passing through disconnected teams.

03 / Operating principles

Credibility is a product decision.

Every public claim should identify whether it is implemented, in development, or long-term research. Product pages lead with the working system; roadmap pages preserve ambition without presenting it as a shipment.
