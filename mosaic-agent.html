04 / Long-term direction

Built toward increasingly general intelligence.

Our long-term goal is an agent that can reason across domains, maintain context over long periods, learn from previous execution patterns, coordinate tools and models, and solve increasingly open-ended objectives with less human supervision. Future releases are planned around a commercial membership and broader intelligence infrastructure, while preserving local execution where it offers meaningful privacy, latency, or control.
