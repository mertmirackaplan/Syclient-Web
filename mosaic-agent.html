04 / Model roadmap

Mosaic Two is in development—not a released model.

Mosaic Two is our agent-focused model direction. We are exploring hybrid Mixture-of-Experts ideas for tool use, verification, and long-running work. There is no public Mosaic Two checkpoint today; near-term progress will come through small fine-tunes, routing experiments, and documented evaluations.
