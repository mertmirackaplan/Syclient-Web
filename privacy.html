DOCUMENT // 15 — PRIVACY POLICY

Zero-Data-Leak Privacy Standard.

Unlike cloud LLM providers that log prompts and harvest repository code, Syclient’s on-device runtime executes 100% offline on your hardware.

COMPLIANCE // GDPR • KVKK • CCPA

01. On-Device Execution & Zero Telemetry Egress

When running Syclient’s 740M foundation weights (including GGUF Q4_K_M quantized builds) on your local workstation, laptop, mobile device, or edge server, zero prompt tokens, code diffs, or file system contents are transmitted to Syclient servers or any third party. All inference resides strictly in local memory.

02. Website & Partnership Inquiry Data

On this web portal (syclient.ai), we collect only the institutional contact details you voluntarily submit via our Strategic Partnership & Compute Allocation form:

  • Full Name and Institutional Email Address
  • Organization / Venture Fund / Compute Cluster Affiliation
  • Collaboration Track and Brief Notes

03. Local Browser Storage & Cookies

We utilize localStorage and sessionStorage strictly to store your cookie consent preference and temporary form confirmation receipts. You may opt into "Essential Only" mode at any time via the Cookie Directive banner.

04. Data Protection Contact

For any privacy, security, or regulatory inquiries, contact our Data Protection & Research Security officer directly at privacy@syclient.ai or founders@syclient.ai.
